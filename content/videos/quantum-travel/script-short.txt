How do you quantum travel in Star Citizen? Four steps. Hold the B key — a real hold, not a tap — until your ship's flight mode reads NAV. Middle-click to switch into quantum-travel mode, then aim your nose at a purple marker. Wait five to fifteen seconds while the drive calibrates on its own. Then hold your left mouse button to jump.

If the drive won't calibrate, you're probably still in a restricted area, or out of quantum fuel. Quantum fuel is a separate tank from your regular fuel, refueled at any Cry-Astro station.
