Want a two-hundred-fifty-dollar ship for sixty-five dollars? It's called a CCU chain, and backers use it to upgrade cheap ships into expensive ones for a fraction of the price.

Here's the trick. A CCU — Cross-Chassis Upgrade — swaps one ship for a pricier one, and you only pay the difference. Chain several together, and you climb from a cheap starter all the way to your dream ship.

The real savings are Warbond CCUs — steps that go on sale fifty to ninety percent off, but only during big events like IAE in November.

Buy every Warbond step you can during a sale, but don't apply any of them yet. Wait until the whole chain is sitting in your hangar, then apply them one at a time, in order.
