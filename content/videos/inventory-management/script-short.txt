Think your cargo just vanished in Star Citizen? Here's what's actually going on, and how to stop it happening again.

Press I to open your inventory. Your personal items show on one side, the ship or station storage on the other. But you can only transfer when you're standing right next to that ship or terminal. Drag items between the two panels, or right-click and choose Transfer All to move a whole stack.

Here's the mistake that costs people the most. If your ship is destroyed and you file an insurance claim, the replacement is a brand new copy. Everything still sitting in the old one — including your cargo — is gone for good. Always unload before you claim.
