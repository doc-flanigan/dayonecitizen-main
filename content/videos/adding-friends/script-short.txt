Want to play Star Citizen with a friend? Here's how to add them, fast.

If you're both already in the game, press F11 to open the Commlink. Click Global Chat, then the Members tab — that lists everyone online with you right now. Click their name and choose Send Friend Request.

If they're standing right next to you in the game world, skip all that. Just hold F to open the Inner Thought wheel, aim at them, and pick Add Contact. No handle needed.

One more thing: being friends doesn't put you on the same server. Press F11, right-click their name, and choose Invite to Party before anyone enters the universe.
