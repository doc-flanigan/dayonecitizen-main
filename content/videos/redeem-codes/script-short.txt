Looking for a working Star Citizen redeem code? Here's the truth: there's no public list, and nothing you paste in today unlocks a free ship.

Real codes only come from two places. A gift code, emailed to you when someone buys you a game package — you apply it yourself in account settings. Or a promo code from Cloud Imperium Games, the studio behind the game. These show up around a live event, entered under RSI Account — that's Roberts Space Industries — then Settings, then Apply a Promotional Code.

That's it. No daily codes, no secret money codes, no free-ship codes outside in-person events.

If you're brand new, skip the code hunt. Use a referral code instead — it adds fifty thousand UEC to your account at signup, guaranteed.
