Did you know your character in Star Citizen can actually starve to death? Here's the one habit that keeps that from ever happening.

Two meters, Nutrition and Hydration, drain over time while you play. Check them on your mobiGlas wrist computer — press F1 — or glance at the bottom-left of your screen. Let either one run out, and you take damage, then fall unconscious.

So always carry at least one food item and one drink in your personal inventory. Take your helmet off first, then eat or drink before you're running low, not after. Plain filtered water is a safe pick — it has a Hydration Efficacy Index of eighty, a solid baseline for staying hydrated.
