Want the best ship setup for a fight? Here's the simple combo.

Grade A power plant — it runs your weapons, shields, and engines at full power, no throttling. Military shields, because combat means absorbing hits, and nothing holds more. A performance cooler to handle the heat from sustained fire. And energy weapons, since a Grade A power plant feeds them full power, they never run out of ammo, and they strip shields fast.

One trade-off: a Grade A power plant has the strongest electromagnetic signal, so you show up bright on enemy radar. If you want that fight, test the full combo first in Arena Commander — the practice mode — before you commit to it for real.
