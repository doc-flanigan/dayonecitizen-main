If you only remember one thing, remember this. Cubby Blast in Area Eighteen has the widest weapon selection anywhere. Hurston Security Depot in Lorville sells the best armor. Shubin Interstellar in New Babbage is your mining gear stop. And you can buy ships with earned money at New Deal in Lorville or Astro Armada in Area Eighteen.

A quick note before we start. Shopping in Star Citizen depends on where you are standing. The exact same item can cost ten to thirty percent more at a small outpost than it does at a city shop. A free website called Gallog dot co lets you compare prices before you fly somewhere expensive. Every city and station I'm about to mention sits in Stanton, the one star system you can currently explore in the Star Citizen alpha.

Let's start with New Babbage, the city on the planet microTech. This is your best stop for mining gear and high-end ship parts. Shubin Interstellar here is the one place that covers every mining laser and mining head you could want. Omega Pro sells some of the best ship components in the system, especially power plants and shields. Tammany and Sons covers general gear, armor, and weapons. The microTech Showroom is where you browse and buy ships in person.

Next is Area Eighteen, the shopping district on ArcCorp. This is the place for personal weapons. Cubby Blast carries ballistic rifles, energy weapons, shotguns, and pistols, plus armor, all under one roof. Dumper's Depot sells ship components and also has locations at most orbital stations, so it is the easiest component shop to reach. Apocalypse Arms specializes in ship-mounted weapons. Astro Armada is where you can buy entire ships using money you earned in-game instead of real money.

Then there's Lorville, on the planet Hurston. This is the best place to buy a ship with earned in-game money. New Deal has the widest ship selection for that kind of purchase, including a few ships you can only get from Hurston Dynamics. Hurston Security Depot sells some of the toughest armor in the game. A few items need good standing with the Hurston faction first. Skutters sells work clothing you cannot find anywhere else.

You'll also find smaller versions of these shops at space stations orbiting each planet. Dumper's Depot and Casaba Outlet, for clothing, show up at most stations, so you rarely need a city trip for basics.

Now, a few things nobody tells you up front. First, stock up on consumables like MedPens, food, and ammo while you are in a city. They're cheaper there than at outposts. Second, shops can run out of specific items entirely. The Star Citizen wiki and Gallog both track what is currently in stock, so check before you make a long trip. Third, some of the best armor and gear requires standing with a specific faction, like Hurston Security Depot's top-tier sets. If a shop won't sell you something, that's probably why.
