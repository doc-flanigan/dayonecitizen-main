Where do you actually buy a weapon in Star Citizen?

Go to Cubby Blast in Area Eighteen, the shopping district on the planet ArcCorp. It has the widest personal weapon selection anywhere — ballistic rifles, energy weapons, shotguns, pistols — plus armor, all in one shop.

Want tougher armor instead? Hurston Security Depot in Lorville sells some of the best protection-rated sets in the game. A few of the top items do require standing with the Hurston faction first.

One more tip: the same gear can cost ten to thirty percent more at a small outpost than in a city. Check prices on Gallog dot co before you fly somewhere expensive.
